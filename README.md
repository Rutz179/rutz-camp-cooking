# Rutz's Camp Cooking

Camp and cooking for Foundry VTT, in any game system. The party makes camp, and everyone's bags go into one pot: a dish comes out, and the diners get a light buff for the day.

Works on its own. With Rutz's PF2e Shop, grocers stock its ingredients.

**Free edition**: the cooking pot, every built-in dish and ingredient, the larder, a simple camp (cook, then pass the night) and the dinner's buff.

**Paid edition** adds: camp jobs (watch, camp site, forage, hunt, fish, mend gear, campfire stories), night events and lost baggage, harvesting after a fight, making and editing dishes, recipe tabs shared between worlds, seasoning effects, a choice of skills for each check, and two more window skins. With Rutz's PF2e Days, it takes over the evening's camp.
